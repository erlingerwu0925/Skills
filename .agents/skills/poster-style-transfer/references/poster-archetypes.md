# Poster Archetypes

Classify the reference by the mechanism that carries attention. Choose one primary archetype and, when useful, one secondary archetype.

## Typography-led

The main title or letterform is the hero. Lock title footprint, line breaks, alignment, spacing, distortion, and negative space before other details.

## Character-led

An illustrated, crafted, or 3D character dominates. Lock silhouette scale, crop, expression energy, material treatment, and title overlap. Replace the character design and theme-specific accessories.

## Portrait-led

A photographed person anchors the poster. Lock framing, body scale, pose energy, lighting, styling density, and relationship to type. Use an original person unless identity preservation is explicitly requested.

## Product-led

A product is the main object. Lock camera angle, product-to-canvas ratio, material realism, grounding shadow, reflection behavior, and commercial lighting. Replace the product and its branded packaging.

## Illustration-led

An illustration carries the composition. Lock line quality, shape language, shading method, palette, and mass distribution. Do not trace a unique illustration.

## Collage

Multiple sources form a constructed image. Lock layer count, cutout treatment, scale jumps, edge behavior, overlap rhythm, and texture unification. Replace every source image and semantic fragment.

## 3D or surreal

Rendered objects or impossible spatial relationships dominate. Lock material family, lighting, depth, camera logic, and surreal transformation rule. Rebuild objects for the new theme.

## Editorial-grid

Information architecture and modular alignment create the identity. Lock grid, margins, column rhythm, text hierarchy, image modules, and caption system. Adapt modules to the new copy length.

## Cinematic or narrative

Scene, atmosphere, and hierarchy communicate a story. Lock viewpoint, depth layers, light direction, tonal curve, and title placement. Replace characters, setting, and narrative symbols.

## Minimal-symbol

One symbol and controlled negative space carry the poster. Lock symbol scale, placement, palette restraint, and typography economy. Avoid filling the empty space with new decoration.

## Hybrid selection

Use a primary plus secondary label when two mechanisms are inseparable, for example `character-led + typography-led`. Assign fidelity priorities from the primary archetype first.
